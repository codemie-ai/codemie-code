# Other languages and non-native English

**Substance is language-independent.** The delete test, the evidence rules, diagnosis before
prescription, the decision test and every gate apply unchanged: a proposal with no price has no
price in any language, and you can see that without fluency.

**Surface is not.** The English word lists do not transfer. The scanner raises a signal when
Latin script falls below 60%; after that, ignore its phrase findings and trust only its
structural metrics (sentence and paragraph length variance, evidence density).

**The competence rule.** Rewrite English and the languages below (Ukrainian, Russian, Polish,
German); in any other language, audit and do not rewrite. Either way, report what you can verify
(the structure, the missing price, the unsupported claims, the same-length sections) and say
which layer you did not judge. A rewrite is in the document's language.

## Translationese: the strongest tell

English sentence shape in another language's words: rigid subject-verb-object order where the
language orders by emphasis; nominal style ("provision of optimisation of the process") where a
verb would carry it; English business idioms translated word for word; English article and
preposition habits; one formality level per sentence instead of per relationship.

Places to look, not proof:

- **Ukrainian:** у сучасному світі, варто зазначити, відіграє ключову роль, комплексне рішення,
  безшовна інтеграція, repeated не лише… але й, даний where цей is natural, verbal nouns where a
  verb would carry it.
- **Russian:** является as a copula (данный подход является эффективным for этот подход
  эффективен), в современном мире, стоит отметить, играет ключевую роль, комплексный подход,
  chains of genitives, stacked participial clauses.
- **Polish:** warto zauważyć, że; w dzisiejszym świecie; kompleksowe rozwiązanie; innowacyjny as
  a default adjective; English clause order in subordinate clauses.
- **German:** Nominalstil ("Die Durchführung der Optimierung"), "Es ist wichtig zu beachten,
  dass" as an opener, innovativ, nahtlos, ganzheitlich, zukunftsweisend, verb-final order broken,
  du and Sie mixed in one document.

## Leave these alone

- **Code-switching.** English technical terms inside a Slavic or German text (deploy, pipeline,
  merge request, release) and phonetically adapted loans are how engineers write, and a strong
  sign a person did. Do not localise them or make a bilingual document monolingual. Choosing the
  language per recipient is normal in multilingual teams.
- **Non-native English.** A dropped article, a different preposition, a construction carried over
  from a first language: a voice, not an AI tell. Generated English is idiomatically perfect, and
  that perfection is itself a signal. Fix only what obscures meaning, keep recurring quirks at the
  same rate, and never normalise someone's English into another person's idiom.
- **Translated documents** carry the source language's structure whether a person or a machine
  translated them. Ask which version is the original, and report structure findings apart from
  any judgment about authorship.
